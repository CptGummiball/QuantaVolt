# License and Permissions

QuantaVolt is **not open source**. All rights belong to cptgummiball. The binding text is the
[QuantaVolt License](https://github.com/CptGummiball/QuantaVolt/blob/main/LICENSE); this page only
summarizes it.

## Allowed without asking

* Playing with QuantaVolt, in singleplayer and on any server.
* Videos, streams, screenshots, reviews and guides.
* **Modpacks**, public or private, free or paid, as long as the QuantaVolt jar files stay exactly as
  published. Linking to the official download is preferred where the platform supports it.
* Writing your own mod that works with QuantaVolt through the [API](For-Mod-Developers), without
  shipping QuantaVolt files in it.

## Not allowed without written permission

* Changing QuantaVolt in any way: patches, forks, ports, re-textures, modified jar files.
* Decompiling or reverse engineering QuantaVolt, except where the law explicitly allows it.
* Uploading QuantaVolt anywhere other than inside a modpack (other websites, launchers, mirrors).
* Using QuantaVolt's textures, models, icons, sounds or texts in other projects.
* Translations (language files or resource packs).
* Putting QuantaVolt classes or files into another mod.

## Asking for permission

Open a **[permission request](https://github.com/CptGummiball/QuantaVolt/issues/new?template=permission_request.yml)**
and describe what you plan. A permission is only valid if cptgummiball grants it in writing in
that issue, and only for what is described there.

## Official downloads

QuantaVolt is only published on Modrinth and CurseForge. Files from other sources are not official
and may be modified.
