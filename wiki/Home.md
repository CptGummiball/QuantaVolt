# QuantaVolt Wiki

Industrial power generation for Minecraft: from a small dynamo to boilers and turbines, gas and combined cycle,
solar, wind and big storage, fission, tokamak fusion, an artificial star and black hole technology. Large plants are
multiblocks you build block by block. QuantaVolt runs on **Fabric** and **NeoForge** and needs
[Pipster](https://modrinth.com/mod/pipster) for its gases.

> **1.0.0** – the first release after the beta. Please report bugs on the
> [issue tracker](https://github.com/CptGummiball/QuantaVolt/issues) with your Minecraft version, loader and log.
